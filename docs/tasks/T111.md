  (`runs/T110/order-r0001.json`, 49 instructions anchored by committed bytes):
  the display order is a per-division sort descending by (points, the signed
  8-bit low byte of F-A, goals for as unsigned 16-bit) - a quicksort on the
  points byte (01EF:4227) then a bubble pass over equal-points neighbours
  (01EF:4305), five groups of 16 (01EF:4450). [confirmed:archaeology T105]

  (`runs/T105/seed-r0001.json`): three points ties, all goal-difference

- **Scope**: [designed] implement the decoded comparator faithfully - the two
  passes over a 16-slot index array (Hoare partition on the points byte with
  the original's mid pivot and descending compares, then the bubble whose
  swaps need equal points and the 8-bit goal-difference low byte strictly
  greater, or those bytes equal and goals for strictly greater as unsigned
  16-bit), returning the positions 1..=16 the original writes to club record
  +0x36. One fixture carries both witnessed weeks (each 16 rows: club, points,
  goals for and against, displayed position) with digests pulled from the
  committed archaeology files. The five-division orchestration, the season
  setup's initial order (`0006:0540` on a `[DS:D7B6]` byte) and anything
  beyond one division of 16 stay out of scope - not witnessed here.

  16, and keep goals for unsigned 16-bit. The quicksort and bubble are the
  original's mechanics, not a clean-room sort: translating them faithfully is
  what makes clubs equal in (points, GD8, goals for) behave as the original's
  arrangement does. No witnessed pair ties that far - say so in the test
  comments rather than inventing a tie-break. Fixture digests come from the
  committed archaeology files, never recomputed from private state.
