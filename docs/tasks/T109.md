  at W0; `record_league_week_2` requires the witnessed score [3,3], return,

  The post-state equals the W2 projection field for field and round-trips
  encode/decode/validate. The availability refresh is the week transaction's
  own pinned output (the T91/T81 boundary), not a modelled rule; no eligibility
  rule is decoded; no third week is claimed.
